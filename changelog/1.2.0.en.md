Your own courses:
- The "+" at the top of your plan adds courses that are not in LSF: once, weekly or every 2 weeks, with subject, color, room, lecturer, note and s.t.
- They can pause in lecture-free weeks; a new import leaves them as they are
- Marked as your own course; edit and delete (with undo) in the details

Your own changes to dates:
- Notes, "s.t.", room and time for one date or all upcoming ones
- Cancel dates for yourself (with undo) or keep only yours with "Choose dates"
- Dashed violet border, LSF changes stay orange; the details show the original values
- They survive a new import; changes whose date is gone are listed in the settings for cleanup

Sharing:
- Your own courses and changes are shared (can be turned off: "Share own changes"), notes never are
- Your friends need version 1.2.0 to see them; older versions ask for an update

View:
- A subtle line shows the current time across the whole week
- Swiping between you and your friends no longer stutters, not even the first time
