New:
- Passing on a renamed friend's plan (file, share, QR code) asks which name to share it under: the original or the local one. Identical names are shared without asking
- A card at the top of the settings points to Mensa MD, the menus of the Magdeburg canteens, as long as the app is not installed: "Download" fetches it from Mensa MD's GitHub releases, checks checksum and signing key and installs it through Android's installer; if anything fails, "Open on GitHub" leads to the release page. Once Mensa MD is installed the card stays away by default. "Show note about Mensa MD" under "Version" switches it on or off at any time (with "Open" when the app is installed); the setting is part of the backup
