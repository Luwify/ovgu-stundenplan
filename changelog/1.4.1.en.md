New:
- Passing on a renamed friend's plan (file, share, QR code) asks which name to share it under: the original or the local one. Identical names are shared without asking
- A card at the bottom of the settings points to Mensa MD, the menus of the Magdeburg canteens: "Open" if the app is installed, otherwise "Download". The download comes from Mensa MD's GitHub releases, is checked for checksum and signing key and installed through Android's installer; if anything fails, "Open on GitHub" leads to the release page. The card can be hidden and switched back on under "Version" with "Show note about Mensa MD"; the setting is part of the backup
