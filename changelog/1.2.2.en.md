Important for friends:
- Please ask friends to update the app too and share their plan again: older versions still count biweekly courses the old way after New Year, so their plans would show those in the wrong weeks

New:
- An .ics file can be added as a friend with "+" in the menu, "Open with" or "Share"; friends imported from an .ics are marked, and updating them keeps name, colors, order and favorite
- Single courses or another group of an exercise can be added to your own plan; they count for "Together with", are marked in the appointment sheet and can be removed
- "Select dates" and "Remove from my plan" affect only that group
- The rhythm of biweekly appointments is shown in the appointment sheet and set under "Edit subject" (even weeks, odd weeks, every 14 days), also in a friend's plan; own courses can follow it too

Changed:
- Your own plan is now replaced only by the import in the settings; if a file has far fewer courses than your plan, the app asks whether to replace or add

Fixed:
- Biweekly courses are in the right weeks after New Year (2026 has a week 53, followed by another odd week 1)
- The hours in the week view sit centered on their line, and the first one is no longer cut off
- The day bar next to the hours starts just above the first hour
