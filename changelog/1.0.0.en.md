The first version of Stundenplan MD:
- Import your timetable from the LSF iCal export; everything stays offline on your phone
- Week and day view, swipe between weeks and days
- Room and time changes, extra appointments and cancellations are highlighted
- Subjects with your own short name and color
- Add friends' plans by QR code or file, see where they are and spot shared events
- Mark favorite friends and reorder friends in the side drawer
- Themes: System, Soft, AMOLED and a custom theme
- Backup and restore through a file
