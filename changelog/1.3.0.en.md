Important:
- Shared plans with hidden dates or per-type short names can only be opened with version 1.3.0 or newer; anyone importing them should update first

New:
- Subjects can be hidden: the whole subject, only one type (e.g. the exercise) or only one group, from the details of a date and in "Edit subject", also in friends' timetables
- Single dates can be hidden ("Hide this date"), in addition to "Cancel for me"
- Hidden things stay hidden after a new import; the new "Hidden" page in the settings lists everything by person, each with "Restore"
- Short names per type: "Short name (all types)" applies to the whole subject, "Short name (this type)" is added to it, e.g. "N4 VL" and "N4 Ex"; with a preview
- Saturday and Sunday appear in the week and day views as soon as something takes place on that day in the week, e.g. an own course

Changed:
- Hidden things don't count for "Together with" and are not shared; friends don't see hidden dates either
- Existing short names now apply to all types of the subject; nothing changes visibly
- The backup also contains what is hidden
- "Choose dates" asks every time what happens to the dates not selected: show as cancelled or hide; hidden dates stay in that list
- Editing opened from the details of a date returns to the details afterwards, showing the new state
- "Edit subject" can edit and delete own courses and remove added ones, also from the subject list in the settings
- "Edit subject": the full course name expands with a tap; each course forms one group with its rhythm; the course menu is now called "Options"
- The name and icons at the top change with your finger while swiping between people
- The automatic update check runs at most every 6 hours instead of once a day, also when the app comes back from the background

Fixed:
- A short, quick swipe over the week grid reliably moves on to the next person
- Notices with "Undo" also appear over the settings
