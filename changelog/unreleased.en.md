New:
- The day view shows breaks of at least 45 minutes between two courses as a dashed placeholder with the time range and duration; hidden dates and dates cancelled for me don't count as courses, also in friends' timetables
- Landscape: the week view is squashed to show the whole day without scrolling; course cards drop lines that no longer fit. On a phone the bars get more compact for this (tabs on the left, one-line week and day rows). Can be turned off in the settings under "Fit to screen in landscape"

Changed:
- The actions in an appointment's details sheet are now one row of icons with short labels: Changes, Skip, Hide and More. "Hide" offers the date, subject, type and group; "More" holds the rest, such as editing the subject, choosing dates, and editing, deleting or removing own and added courses
- The "Hide" and "More" menus grow upward out of their button with a slightly springy motion, and the button stays visible; thin lines separate the entries

Fixed:
- The details sheet of an appointment wobbled up and down after a small drag down and release when its content nearly filled the screen height (as with the longer German labels); it now settles calmly. In landscape it also no longer keeps bouncing after scrolling all the way down
- Changing the week and then right away swiping up or down stuttered; friends' timetables now catch up with the new week between gestures
- Choosing a friend further down in the menu sometimes landed on the friend before them
