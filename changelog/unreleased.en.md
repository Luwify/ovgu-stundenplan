New:
- Subjects can be hidden: in the details of a date and in "Edit subject", the whole subject, only one type (e.g. only the exercise) or only one group, also in friends' timetables. Hidden things stay saved and don't come back after a new import
- Single dates can be hidden completely ("Hide this date"), in addition to "Cancel for me"
- New "Hidden" page in the settings: everything hidden, by person, each with "Restore"
- Short names per type: "Short name (all types)" applies to the whole subject, "Short name (this type)" is added to it, e.g. "N4 VL" and "N4 Ex"; with a preview
- Saturdays and Sundays appear in the week and day views as soon as something takes place on that day in the week (e.g. an own course on the weekend), for you and for friends
- "Choose dates" asks every time what happens to the dates not selected: show as cancelled or hide; hidden dates stay in that list to choose again

Changed:
- Hidden things don't count for "Together with" and are not shared; friends don't see hidden dates either
- The name at the top changes with your finger while swiping between people, not only afterwards
- Existing short names now apply to all types of the subject; nothing changes visibly
- The backup also contains what is hidden
- Editing opened from the details of a date (note & changes, edit subject, choose dates, edit course) returns to the details after saving, cancelling or going back, showing the new state; only hiding and removing close them
- In the subject overview, own courses can also be edited and deleted and added courses removed, whatever the day of the week
- "Edit subject": the full course name below the short name fields expands and collapses with a tap; each course forms one group with its rhythm rows; a course's menu is called "Options"
- The icons at the top right change together with the name while swiping between people; the name no longer jumps at the end of a swipe
- A quick swipe over the week grid moves on to the next person, even when it is only a short swipe
- Notices with "Undo" also appear over the settings
- The automatic update check runs at most every 6 hours instead of once a day, also when the app comes back from the background; the notice about a new version still appears only once
