Your own changes to your plan:
- Notes for one date or all upcoming dates; a small note icon shows them in the week and day view
- Mark "s.t." for one date or all upcoming ones (c.t. stays the default)
- Cancel a date for yourself (with undo), or keep only your dates of a lab course with "Choose dates"
- Change the room and time of one date or all upcoming ones
- Your changes have a dashed violet border, LSF changes stay orange; the details say "Changed by you" with the original values
- They survive a new import; if LSF changes the same date, you see both. Changes whose date is gone are listed in the settings for cleanup
- Cancellations, rooms, times and s.t. are shared (can be turned off: "Share own changes"), notes never are. Older app versions then need an update to open the plan

Week view:
- A subtle line shows the current time across the whole week, behind the events
