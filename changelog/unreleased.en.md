New:
- "Add to my plan" on a friend's appointment copies the whole series of that group into the own timetable, with undo. The appointments from LSF are copied, not the friend's changes, notes or hidden dates. A later import of the own calendar file with that course replaces the copy instead of doubling it. Another group of a subject is added as an extra group, a friend's own course as an own course. If the subject is already in the plan it keeps its color, otherwise the color comes from the friend's plan. If the group is already in the plan, it says "In your plan"; a group copied this way can be taken out again with "Remove"

Fixed:
- In the week view the icons at the top right (together, changed, note, own course) took space over the whole height, so long room names were cut off. Now only the lines next to the icons are narrower
- In messages over two lines, "Undo" now sits centered next to the text instead of level with the bottom line
