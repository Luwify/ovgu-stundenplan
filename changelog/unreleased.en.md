Fixed:
- When entering times, the colon could be deleted but not typed back on the number keyboard. Now only digits are typed and the colon stays fixed in the field: 1015 becomes 10:15, 915 becomes 09:15. Invalid times like 24:00 or 12:60 are marked as errors and left as typed. An hour alone (such as 9) is no longer enough, the minutes are needed too
- The clock icon next to each time field now opens the time picker with a dial or keyboard input

Web version:
- Times are now entered with the browser's own time input, on the iPad with the system's time wheel
