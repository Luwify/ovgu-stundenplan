Important:
- QR codes from version 1.5.0 can only be scanned with 1.5.0 or newer: anyone who should receive a timetable by QR code should update the app first. Sharing as a file still works with older versions too

New:
- Sharing by QR code now sends the whole timetable, nothing shortened, with all notes. If it doesn't fit comfortably into one code, the app shows a sequence of small changing codes; the order doesn't matter when scanning, and the progress is shown in percent
- Below the QR code, "As one code" or "Animated" can be chosen; the choice is remembered

Changed:
- Messages at the bottom can be swiped away to the left or right; this doesn't undo anything. They follow the finger and fade out, a short drag lets them snap back
- The messages have clear contrast in every color scheme and are centered and narrower on wide screens. Long messages show two lines and an arrow; a tap expands the whole text and collapses it again. Messages with "Undo" stay for 10 seconds, plain info ones shorter, and longer while they are expanded or touched
- Choice menus in the settings (language, color scheme) and the subject picker for an own course open like a classic Android spinner: the current value sits exactly on the field, the other values above and below it. Near the screen edges the menu moves only as far as needed. It grows out of the field with a slight spring and closes at once
- The note "The QR code doesn't contain all details" is gone, since nothing is shortened any more. QR codes of older versions can still be scanned

Fixed:
- In landscape on a phone, messages sat needlessly high above the bottom edge
